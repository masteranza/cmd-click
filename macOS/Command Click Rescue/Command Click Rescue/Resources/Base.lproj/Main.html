Command Click Rescue

Your click. A new tab.
Your place, preserved.

Checking Safari extension…

  1. Enable in Safari.Open Safari Settings → Extensions and turn on Command Click Rescue.
  2. Choose your websites.Allow access to the sites you want to protect. Choose All Websites to use it everywhere Safari permits.
  3. Hold ⌘ and click a link.Safari opens it in a new tab while you stay on the page. Normal clicks work as usual.

Safari Settings couldn’t open. Open Safari → Settings → Extensions manually.

Free. No tracking. Everything happens on your Mac.

Help & limitations

Reload a website after enabling the extension or changing access. Protection applies to real HTTP and HTTPS links, including links in frames and shadow DOM where Safari permits the extension to run. Download links and buttons without a link destination are left alone. Some sites run scripts before the extension and may still override a click. Safari controls whether new tabs open in the foreground or background. You can quit this app after setup; the extension keeps working.

Support: github.com/masteranza/cmd-click/issues

Privacy policy

Command Click Rescue does not collect, store, or transmit personal data, browsing history, or website content. On websites you allow, it examines the link under a Command-click to preserve Safari’s normal link action. This processing stays on your Mac. There are no analytics, advertising, accounts, or third-party SDKs. Change or revoke website access in Safari Settings → Extensions, or disable the extension at any time. This policy covers the app and extension, not the websites you visit. Effective October 1, 2026.
