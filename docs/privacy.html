Privacy policy

Your browsing
stays yours.

Effective October 1, 2026 · Developer: Michał Mandrysz

The app and Safari extension do not collect, store, or transmit personal data, browsing history, or website content.

What the extension does

On websites you allow, Command Click Rescue observes mouse events and examines the link under a Command-click. It checks whether that link points to an HTTP or HTTPS destination and stops interfering page event listeners so Safari can perform its normal link action. This processing happens locally on your Mac.

No tracking or data collection

The app and extension have no analytics, advertising, tracking, user accounts, third-party SDKs, or developer-operated backend. They do not save click activity or send page contents, URLs, or browsing history to the developer.

Your controls

Grant or revoke access to individual websites in Safari → Settings → Extensions. You can disable the extension or uninstall the app at any time. You can close the companion app after setup; Safari runs the extension.

This website and support

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under GitHub’s privacy statement. This website adds no analytics, cookies, or third-party fonts. If you choose to submit a GitHub issue, its contents are public and GitHub processes your account and submitted information. Do not include sensitive information in an issue.

Scope and changes

This policy covers Command Click Rescue’s app, Safari extension, and this support website. Websites you browse have their own privacy policies. If the app’s data handling changes, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the developer through the project’s GitHub issues.
